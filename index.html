Course Overview

BUS 100 introduces students to contemporary business and helps first-year students connect with the Scott College of Business through exploration of accounting, finance, marketing, management, supply chain management, HR, professional development, teamwork, ethics, and AI.


Required Text

Better Business (7th Edition) by Solomon, Poatsy & Martin

Learning Objectives

Apply academic success strategies.
Develop academic and career planning skills.
Demonstrate professional communication.
Apply critical thinking and AI tools.
Understand major business disciplines.
Analyze environmental influences on business.
Evaluate ethics, diversity, and inclusion.
